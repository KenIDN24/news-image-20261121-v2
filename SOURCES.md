# Sources — THE WORLD IN ONE DAY, Saturday 21 November 2026

Every story on the page was reported on 21 November 2026. Headlines and
captions on the page are my own wording of the facts below, drawn from
reporting by Reuters, the BBC, the Wall Street Journal, ISW, the United
Nations and Al Jazeera.

## Lead

1. **Missiles down in Poland** — Russian cruise missiles and a drone fell
   inside Polish territory close to a coalition exercise area. Prime
   Minister Tusk publicly called the incident a deliberate provocation,
   and NATO Secretary General Rutte travelled to Warsaw the same day.

## Around the world

2. **Kyiv bombardment** — Ukraine reported the heaviest strike of
   November: 27 ballistic missiles, 84 cruise missiles and 404 drones,
   515 weapons in total. Three bridges over the Dnipro were damaged and
   at least nine people were killed.
3. **UK–EU reset** — London and Brussels signed a reset package:
   100,000 youth visas in each direction, the end of food checks, a
   £17bn deal, and the UK rejoining Erasmus+.
4. **Markets** — An AI-driven selloff wiped about $4 trillion from
   markets; the Nasdaq lost 5.3% in its worst week since 2020, and
   Anthropic fell 8.5% a day after its IPO.
5. **COP31** — The climate summit ran into overtime without a deal
   after fossil fuels were removed from the draft text. A third draft
   was due on Saturday, with Panama threatening to block agreement.
6. **Denmark plot** — Danish authorities foiled a Russian plot to
   assassinate the Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner; a Russian attaché was
   expelled and given seven days to leave.
7. **Hormuz tanker** — A tanker was hit by a projectile and caught fire
   off Khasab, Oman, at the Strait of Hormuz. The crew were reported
   safe.

## In brief

8. **Peru quake** — A magnitude 6.8 earthquake, 129 km deep, struck
   Junín; Lima residents rushed into the streets and no casualties were
   reported.
9. **India** — Congress swept Rajasthan, winning 33 of 37 seats, as
   Prime Minister Modi opened an SCO forum.
10. **United States** — A federal shutdown loomed as Congress split
    over $350bn of funding for the Iran war.
11. **Science** — Two candidate proofs of the Riemann hypothesis
    surfaced in the same week, one AI-assisted and one the product of
    twelve years of human work.
12. **Sport** — Lauren Price became the first Welsh boxer to be an
    undisputed champion.
13. **Health** — Measles cases in the United States approached 6,000.
14. **New York / Washington** — Mayor Mamdani pledged to close Rikers
    Island by 2032, and the Pentagon released further UFO footage.
